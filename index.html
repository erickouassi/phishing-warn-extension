🛡️ Real-Time Alerts

Automatically scans every site you visit to detect brand impersonation, deceptive URLs, and malicious structures instantly.

🔍 Typosquatting Fix

Intercepts mistyped domains and suggests the correct official site before you accidentally land on a fake portal.

🔒 Privacy First

Zero data tracking. Your browsing activity stays private because URL evaluation happens strictly locally on your device.

⚙️ Custom Safe & Block Lists

Take complete control by manually whitelisting your favorite local sites or blocking unwanted domains with a single click.

🎨 At-a-Glance Status

The extension badge instantly updates its color and icon so you know if a domain is safe, suspicious, or blocked before you interact with it.

🚨 One-Click Threat Report

Spotted a new scam? Report dangerous phishing domains directly from the popup menu to keep the web safe for everyone.
